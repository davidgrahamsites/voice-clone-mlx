VoiceCloneMLX / six-block recording plan

Five minutes at a time.

One idea: record the same complete dataset in six short, comfortable files. Each block has a clean beginning, a known prompt range, and a clear place to stop.

Before every block

  1. Use the same room, recorder position, distance, and gain.
  2. Record 30 seconds of room tone with the air conditioner unchanged.
  3. Wait three seconds, read the calibration passage, then wait two seconds.
  4. Read only the sentences in that block. Do not speak the prompt IDs.
  5. Pause silently for two seconds after every numbered reading.
  6. Stop after the final prompt and its two-second pause. Never stop mid-sentence.

The pause rule

After every sentence, silently count “one-Mississippi, two-Mississippi,” then continue. The three-second pause is only after a spoken style marker. If you make a mistake, stop, wait two seconds, and reread the entire sentence.
